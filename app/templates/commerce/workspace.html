MAKE SENSE OF YOUR NUMBERS

A little stock know-how.

On hand vs. available {{ icon('plus') }}

On hand is the stock recorded at your location. Available is on hand minus reserved units. You currently have {{ stock.available }} available units from counted variants.

What is stock value? {{ icon('plus') }}

Each counted variant’s on-hand quantity multiplied by its selling price, added together. It is not sales revenue, profit, or the cost you paid.

Why count zero stock? {{ icon('plus') }}

A zero count confirms that you checked the item and have none. An uncounted item is still unknown and is excluded from totals.
